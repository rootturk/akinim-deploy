Twitch’te rawscorp olarak yayın açıyorum — oyunlar, IRL, korku geceleri, genel internet muhabbeti. Plansız, eğlenceli. Yaklaşık iki yıldır bu community var ve güzel gidiyor.

YouTube’da da zaman zaman bir şeyler çıkıyorum. Öğrendiklerimi, deneyimlerimi, aklımdakileri paylaşıyorum.

Bu site bir blog değil tam olarak. Yazmak istediğimde yazıyorum.

cat bio.md

It started in 2004 with a magazine — there was a section called “let’s build your own website.” That was it. Been coding ever since, first as a hobby, then professionally. The curiosity in the middle never changed.

Technologies shifted, infrastructure changed, new languages came and went. I adapted. C#, Java, Python, PHP, JavaScript, Go when needed — I use whatever fits the problem. Being polyglot wasn’t a choice, it just happened from always looking at the problem first, not the tool.

I did my master’s in cybersecurity. Defense, vulnerability research, CTF — I do these for fun, not because I have to. There’s something satisfying about being able to both build things and break them.

I stream on Twitch as rawscorp — gaming, IRL, horror nights, general internet hangout. No real plan, just good vibes. Been at it for about two years now.

I also post things on YouTube occasionally — stuff I’ve learned, experiences, whatever’s on my mind.

This isn’t really a blog. I write when I feel like it.
